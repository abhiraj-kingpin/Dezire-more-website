# Dezire More — Phone-Optimized Product Photography Brief

**Scope: social/sharing assets only** (Instagram Reels, WhatsApp, Telegram). This is a
**separate** spec from the in-app product grid photos — the product grid still uses the
3:4 portrait / 4:3 landscape standard documented in the admin panel upload guidance
(`dezire-backend/admin-panel.html`, added in the "Add photo/video upload guidance to
admin panel" commit). Do not shoot 9:16 for product-grid listing photos; use this spec
only when producing content for Reels/WhatsApp/Telegram sharing.

For Vertical 9:16 Saree & Ethnic Wear Showcase.

## Shoot Specifications

**Resolution & Aspect Ratio**
- Shoot at 1080×1920px minimum (9:16 perfect vertical for phone)
- OR 3240×5760px (3× upscaled for flexibility)
- This is the ONLY format — no cropping, no adjustments needed

**Why 9:16?**
- Instagram Reels native
- WhatsApp/Telegram perfect fit
- Full phone screen utilization
- Sarees look elegant stretched vertically

## Composition for Sarees/Ethnic Wear

**Full Body Shot Requirements**
- Model standing straight, facing camera
- Head to toe FULLY VISIBLE with small margin
- Head ~150–200px from top edge
- Feet ~100px from bottom edge
- Saree/garment centered horizontally

**Framing Formula**
- Top margin: 5% (for title/price text overlay space)
- Subject: 90% of frame
- Bottom margin: 5%

**Pose**
- ✅ Relaxed standing
- ✅ One hand on hip OR at side (natural)
- ✅ Slight shoulder angle (not rigid)
- ✅ Eyes to camera
- ✅ Let saree fabric drape naturally

## Background (Critical for Phone)

- SOLID COLOR ONLY (Beige, cream, light gray, soft white)
- NO patterns, NO cluttered elements
- Minimal depth (flat background preferred)
- Ensures saree/embroidery stands out sharply
- No shadow confusion on small phone screen

## Lighting (For Phone Screens)

- BRIGHT, EVEN lighting (crucial for small screens)
- Natural daylight OR ring light
- NO harsh shadows on embroidery
- Saree colors pop clearly
- Embroidery/zari details 100% visible

**Avoid**
- Backlighting (silhouettes on phone look bad)
- Side harsh shadows
- Dim/moody lighting (doesn't work on mobile)

## Saree-Specific Checklist

**✅ Must Do**
- [ ] Full saree length visible
- [ ] Pleats/drape shows clearly
- [ ] Embroidery/border patterns sharp & bright
- [ ] Blouse neckline visible
- [ ] Saree pallu visible (if applicable)
- [ ] No fabric cropped at edges
- [ ] Colors accurate (not washed out)

**❌ Never**
- Cropped shots
- Saree folded/bunched awkwardly
- Poor color balance
- Blurry embroidery details
- Model cutting off shoulders/feet

## File Delivery Format

- Filename: `[product-name]_9-16_1080x1920.jpg`
- Format: JPG (compressed <500KB for fast WhatsApp/Telegram send)
- Color Profile: sRGB (phone standard)
- No text overlays on image itself (price/details added in-app separately)

## Before Sending to Client

Check on actual phone:
- [ ] Open image on WhatsApp/Telegram — does it fit perfectly?
- [ ] No cropping at top or bottom?
- [ ] No stretched/distorted look?
- [ ] Embroidery/colors clear on small screen?
- [ ] Can see entire saree length without scrolling?

## Workflow

1. Shoot in 9:16 → centered, full-body
2. Export as 1080×1920px JPG
3. Test on phone (send to yourself first)
4. Use for Reels/WhatsApp/Telegram sharing (not the in-app product grid)
5. Done ✅
